Privacy Policy

At MusiKao (developed by Ae Ja Thwee), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless karaoke and music playback experience, MusiKao may collect or access the following types of data:

  • Audio & Microphone Access: The app requires access to your device's microphone and audio recording capabilities only when you use the recording or karaoke singing features.
  • Storage & Local Files: The app accesses local device storage (via MediaStore or scoped storage) to read audio files, music tracks, and .lrc lyric files that you choose to play or import.
  • Device & Log Information: Basic crash logs and performance diagnostics may be collected anonymously to help us fix bugs and improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To play music tracks, mix audio, and synchronize lyrics in real-time.
  • To allow you to record your singing sessions and save them locally on your device.
  • To improve app performance, troubleshoot technical issues, and enhance user experience.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • Audio recordings and music files processed within the app remain stored locally on your device unless explicitly shared by you.

4. Data Security

We implement standard technical measures to protect your data. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

MusiKao is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this page with an updated effective date.
